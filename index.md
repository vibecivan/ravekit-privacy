# RaveKit Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

_Last updated: October 15, 2026 · 最後更新:2026 年 10 月 15 日_

## English

RaveKit does not collect any data. Period.

- **No data collection.** The app has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no user accounts.
- **No network access.** RaveKit makes zero network requests. Everything runs entirely on your device.
- **Microphone (optional).** Auto Beat Sync and the Sound Meter listen through the microphone, only while you have one of them turned on. Sound is analysed in memory, in real time, on your device. It is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.
- **Motion sensors.** The Glow Stick reads your phone's motion on the device to set its brightness. This data is not stored.
- **Notifications (optional).** Water reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They run for one 8-hour session and then stop.
- **Stored on your device only.** RaveKit keeps a few settings on your device: your preferences, today's water count, your countdown and your marquee text. They are kept in local storage (UserDefaults), never leave your device, and are deleted when you delete the app.
- **No third parties.** There are no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Because no data is collected, none is shared, sold, or transferred — to anyone, ever.

## 繁體中文

RaveKit 不蒐集任何資料。就這麼簡單。

- **不蒐集資料。** 本 App 沒有分析工具、沒有廣告、沒有當機回報、也沒有使用者帳號。
- **不連網。** RaveKit 完全不發出任何網路請求,一切都在你的裝置上運作。
- **麥克風(選用)。** 「自動跟拍」與「分貝計」只在你開啟時透過麥克風聆聽;聲音只在裝置上即時於記憶體中分析,絕不錄音、儲存或傳送。
- **動作感測。** 「螢光棒」在裝置上讀取手機動作來調整亮度,不會儲存。
- **通知(選用)。** 補水提醒只在你的裝置上排程,持續一次 8 小時的時段後自動停止。
- **僅儲存在你的裝置上。** App 只在本機儲存少量設定:偏好設定、今天的喝水杯數、倒數計時與跑馬燈文字。這些資料存放於裝置本機(UserDefaults),永遠不會離開你的裝置,刪除 App 即一併刪除。
- **沒有第三方。** 不含任何第三方 SDK。

因為根本不蒐集資料,所以也不存在任何分享、販售或轉移。
