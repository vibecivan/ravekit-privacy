# RaveKit Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

_Last updated: July 16, 2026 · 最後更新:2026 年 7 月 16 日_

## English

RaveKit does not collect any data. Period.

- **No data collection.** The app has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no user accounts.
- **No network access.** RaveKit makes zero network requests. Everything runs entirely on your device.
- **Local settings only.** The only information the app stores is your in-app preferences (for example, that you have acknowledged the photosensitivity warning). This is kept in your device's local storage (UserDefaults), never leaves your device, and is deleted when you delete the app.
- **No third parties.** There are no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Because no data is collected, none is shared, sold, or transferred — to anyone, ever.

## 繁體中文

RaveKit 不蒐集任何資料。就這麼簡單。

- **不蒐集資料。** 本 App 沒有分析工具、沒有廣告、沒有當機回報、也沒有使用者帳號。
- **不連網。** RaveKit 完全不發出任何網路請求,一切都在你的裝置上運作。
- **僅儲存本機設定。** App 唯一儲存的資訊是你的偏好設定(例如你已閱讀光敏性癲癇警告),存放於裝置本機(UserDefaults),永遠不會離開你的裝置,刪除 App 即一併刪除。
- **沒有第三方。** 不含任何第三方 SDK。

因為根本不蒐集資料,所以也不存在任何分享、販售或轉移。
